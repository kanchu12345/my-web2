1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small data files placed on your browser or device when you visit a website. They allow the website to recognize your device, remember preferences (such as language selection or dark/light mode), and ensure smooth navigational performance.

3. How to Block Cookies via Browser Settings

Most modern web browsers allow you to control cookies through their privacy settings. You can configure your browser to block third-party cookies, alert you before receiving a cookie, or wipe existing cookies whenever you close the browser.
