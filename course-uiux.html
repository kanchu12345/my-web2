1. Introduction to Color Theory

Module 1 of 4 • 10 min read

Color is one of the most powerful tools in a designer's toolkit. It can draw attention, set a mood, influence users' emotions, perceptions, and actions.

Primary, Secondary, and Tertiary Colors

The color wheel consists of three primary colors (Red, Yellow, Blue), three secondary colors (created when primary colors are mixed), and six tertiary colors (colors made from primary and secondary colors).

Pro Tip: 60-30-10 Rule
Use the 60-30-10 rule for color schemes: 60% of the UI should be your dominant color (usually a neutral background), 30% a secondary color (cards, sidebars), and 10% an accent color (buttons, CTAs).

Contrast and Accessibility

When selecting colors, contrast is critical for usability. Text must have sufficient contrast against its background to be readable. The Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) require a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1 for normal text.

Summary

Color choices dictate how a user feels on your website. In the next chapter, we will learn how Typography affects readability and aesthetics.
